Meet the team

The people behind TeachOrange, bringing engaged teaching activities to course instructors.

Mark Brockway

Assistant Teaching Professor, Political Science

Mark Brockway is an Assistant Teaching Professor in Political Science at Syracuse University. He has developed many effective strategies for increasing student engagement and learning in and out of the classroom. He is especially interested in expanding opportunities for civic engagement, giving every student the chance to do an internship, and structuring curricula and degree programs to better serve student goals. He also studies religion in American politics. You can read more on his personal website markbrockway.com

Namratha Tiptur Manjunath

Website & platform development

Namratha Tiptur Manjunath designed and built the TeachOrange website and platform. A data engineer and machine learning enthusiast pursuing a Master's at Syracuse University, Namratha works with healthcare data, builds ETL pipelines, and designs machine learning models. Before graduate school, Namratha spent a year as a data engineer at Carelon Global Solutions in Bangalore, and most recently interned as a Data and Research Intern at the Centre for Public Impact, building data pipelines and dashboards that help government and philanthropic partners make sense of survey data.
